# Regional holiday calendars

Select the region whose holiday schedule you want to follow. Chrono Notes stores these dates in the plugin, so they work offline. Each selection follows the authority and scope listed below. Traditional festivals belong to the separate calendar extensions.

Names follow the effective Chrono Notes interface language. Chinese interfaces display the matching script. A matching local language displays the original holiday name, including Portuguese for Macao and French for Québec. Other languages display the English name.

¹ The Mainland China historical records also include 29 and 30 December 2001, both adjusted working days for New Year 2002. The rest of 2001 has no records.

The Japanese schedule adapts the Cabinet Office’s [National Holidays dataset (CC BY)](https://data.e-gov.go.jp/data/dataset/cao_20190522_0002/resource/d9ad35a5-6c9c-4127-bdbe-aa138fdffe42). Dates are grouped by holiday and the displayed names include translations.

As of 27 November 2026, the official 2028 schedules for Japan, Hong Kong and South Korea are not yet available. Japan expects publication in February 2027; Hong Kong expects it around May 2027. South Korea’s preliminary almanac is not used. Taiwan’s 2028 government office calendar is also pending, with publication due by 30 June 2027 under the issuing agency’s rule. The 31 December 2027 substitute for New Year 2028 stays in the 2027 calendar. Singapore, Macao and Spain also have no official 2028 schedule in the sources used here. Singapore’s Ministry of Manpower publishes by the third quarter of the preceding year; Spain’s nationwide comparison needs the annual BOE table for all 19 jurisdictions. Macao’s separate mandatory-holiday list does not replace its broader public-holiday schedule.

The 2028 [Mainland China holiday and working-weekend arrangements](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/lyj/1/szxx/20261105/695102.html), [Russian government day transfers](https://www.cbr.ru/other/holidays/), [Delhi/New Delhi central government gazetted holidays](https://www.csir.res.in/en/Notification/holidays-be-observed-central-government-offices-during-year-2027-reg) and [Vietnamese public-employee holiday arrangements](https://baochinhphu.vn/nghi-tet-am-lich-2027-keo-dai-7-ngay-nghi-quoc-khanh-4-ngay-102261010095648278.htm) have yet to be published. Viet Nam’s 2027 schedule includes 27 July under the [Labour Code amendment](https://baochinhphu.vn/quoc-hoi-dong-y-bo-sung-2-ngay-nghi-le-duoc-huong-nguyen-luong-102261121121448218.htm) effective 1 March 2027.

Nationwide compulsory rest on that date still needs verification before 2028 can be marked covered. ## Checking a date

Start with the region and civil year. For example, Mid-Autumn Festival is on 15 September 2027. Taiwan’s government offices observe the holiday that day, and Hong Kong’s public holiday is on 16 September. The US federal calendar shows 31 December 2027 as the observed holiday for New Year 2028. Only recorded dates receive holiday labels. A few dates from a neighbouring year’s schedule can appear even when the full year is unavailable.

If an authority changes a date, include its announcement, the region, the calendar date and the expected label when reporting the change. Maintainers update the dates, substitutes, working days and source verification together. The annual check reviews the current and following years. [Annual maintenance](testing-strategy.md)
